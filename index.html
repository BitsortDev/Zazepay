Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Which cryptocurrencies can I trade?

We list USDT, USDC, BTC, SOL and TRX. Please message us to confirm current availability and supported networks.

How do I know the rate?

Our team will confirm the current rate with you directly before you agree to a trade. Rates can change with the market.

When should I send my crypto?

Only send crypto after you have confirmed the amount, network, wallet address and all trade details with our team.

How do I receive payment when I sell?

Payment arrangements are confirmed with you before the trade. Your crypto transfer must be verified before the transaction is marked complete.

Can a crypto transfer be reversed?

Most cryptocurrency transfers cannot be reversed. Always double-check the wallet address and network before sending.
